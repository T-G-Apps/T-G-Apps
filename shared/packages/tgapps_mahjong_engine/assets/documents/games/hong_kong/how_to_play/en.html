How to Play Hong Kong Mahjong

Fast, classic Mahjong scored in faan.

The Goal

Be the first to complete a hand of four sets (chows, pungs or kongs) and a pair, worth at least the minimum faan chosen in Settings (default 3). Play the number of hands set in Settings; the highest score at the end wins.

Setup

144 tiles: three suits (dots, bamboo, characters), winds, dragons and 8 bonus tiles. Each player gets 13 tiles; the dealer (East) starts with 14. The dealer stays as dealer after winning or a drawn hand; otherwise the deal passes on.

Playing

  • On your turn you draw a tile, then discard one. Tap a tile once to lift it and tap it again to discard.
  • When another player discards, you may claim the tile: Chow (a run of three in one suit, only from the player before you), Pung (three identical tiles) or Kong (four identical tiles, which also gives you a replacement tile). A win beats a pung/kong, and a pung/kong beats a chow.
  • You can also declare a concealed kong from four identical tiles in your hand, or add a fourth tile to one of your pungs.
  • Flowers and seasons are bonus tiles: they are set aside automatically and replaced from the end of the wall.

Robots discard to keep their hand as few tiles from ready as possible, counting the tiles still unseen that would improve it. When another player looks close to winning and their own hand is far from ready, they play safe, starting with tiles that player threw away. The Hint shows the same counts for your discard, and highlights the tile.

A hand needs the minimum faan to win, so robots also weigh what each hand would score. They aim for a plan, such as Mixed One Suit, All Pungs or a dragon pung, and give up a plan worth more when it is too many tiles away. The Hint names the plan and what it would score. Seven Pairs can’t claim discards, so robots count it for less the further it is from ready.

Winning and Scoring

Declare Mahjong on a self-drawn tile or on another player's discard. Faan come from patterns such as Self-Drawn (1), Concealed Hand (1), dragon, seat-wind and prevailing-wind pungs (1 each), Common Hand (1), All Pungs (3), Mixed One Suit (3), Pure One Suit (7), Small/Great Three Dragons (5/8), Small/Great Four Winds (6/13), All Honors (10), Thirteen Orphans (13) and matching flowers (1 each). The limit is 13 faan, or the Faan Cap the host chooses.

A discard win is paid by the discarder; a self-drawn win is paid by all three opponents.

Tips

  • Collect dragon and wind pungs early – they add faan quickly.
  • Stay concealed when you can: a concealed self-drawn win is worth 3 faan.
  • Use the Hint button for a suggested discard.

Table Options

The host chooses these in Settings, and every phone at the table shows them under the wall count when they are not the usual ones.

  • Faan Cap: the most faan a hand can score: 8, 10 or 13 (usual). A hand at the cap is a limit hand.
  • Full Doubling: payments double with every faan (1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and so on). Off (usual): they double up to 4 faan, then rise by half steps (24, 32, 48, 64 and so on).

Robots weigh what a hand is worth under the cap and payments in force.
